Case studies

How I work a problem

Twin‑size traffic on Amazon

Lower marketing cost

Problem

Listings kept going ineligible. Twin‑size ad groups underperformed, and King and Queen ads switched on and off, leaving spend in Amazon’s hands.

Hypothesis

Show King beds zoomed to about ¾ of the frame and move the size to the end of Twin titles. Twin’s low price pulls traffic that then buys other sizes.

Actions

  • Competitor keywords from AmzTrends plus high‑conversion search terms for the front end; the full set through Helium 10 for the back end.
  • King, Queen and material keywords in Twin bullet points; King and Queen exact match from Twin campaigns.
  • Twin titles end in “… + Color + Size”.

Forecast demand automation

Inventory planning
Saved per person, every forecast cycle
~1 hour

Situation

Each sales owner ran rolling and demand forecasts by hand. It took hours, and judgment was pulled by marketing fees, blocked sales and ineligible items.

Hypothesis

Automating the demand forecast removes personal bias and cuts the risk of running out of stock.

Expected result

  • No more hand‑built rolling forecasts for each owner.
  • One logic across all products, fewer errors.
  • Less slow‑moving stock from ratios set on history.

Multi‑marketplace dashboard

Looker Studio
One view of profit
Every product, every channel

What it tracks

  • Sales value, revenue, average price, marketplace fees, cost of goods, discounts, returns and ads.
  • Stock on hand, container estimates, incoming purchase orders.
  • Market share by product, color, size and channel.

Why

Profit and loss for every product, so each marketing action is judged against profit, and stock issues show up before they cost a listing.
